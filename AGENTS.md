# OrgPilot repository instructions

- Follow [Coding conventions](docs/CODING_CONVENTIONS.md) when introducing or changing code.
- Follow the accepted [implementation contract](docs/Analytics/docs/15-implementation-contract.md), [domain model](docs/Analytics/docs/16-domain-model.md), and applicable ADRs for architecture and behavior.
- Follow the scope and completion criteria of the current [development stage](docs/DEVELOPMENT_STAGES.md). Do not turn an open architecture decision into an accepted one through implementation.
